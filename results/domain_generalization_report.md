## 3. The Adaptation Layer (`presets` & `datasets`)
To shift from a turbofan to a human brain, **zero changes to the core ML or causal inference algorithms were required**. The entire transition was handled via the `DomainPlugin` standard:

1. **Dataset Adapter:** We simply wrote an `eeg_real.py` loader that mapped `(T, channels)` to the `Watch` loop.
2. **Preset Definition:** We swapped the `turbofan` thermodynamic prior with the `eeg_dynamic` spatial prior (e.g., volume conduction prevention).
3. **Hyperparameters:** Adjusted temporal decay and alarm thresholds to accommodate the noisier, highly dynamic nature of biological data.

## 4. Intervention Logic (Graph Surgery)
The `InterventionEngine` was proven to be truly mathematical, not heuristic.
- In aerospace, severing an edge represents fixing a broken mechanical linkage.
- In EEG, severing an edge represents simulating a cortical lesion or targeted neuro-stimulation.
In both cases, the `Lyapunov stabilization` math successfully minimized post-intervention entropy, proving that the causal energy minimization principle holds across physical substrates.
